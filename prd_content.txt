Product Requirements Document (PRD):
Full-Stack & AI Learning Management
System (LMS)
Goal: To deliver a highly engaging and effective learning experience that produces
portfolio-ready technical talent using a Project-Based Learning (PBL) model, granular
tracking, and advanced AI integration.

key components. Note: These descriptions require companion artifacts (wireframes and
flowcharts) to be generated and maintained alongside the PRD.
